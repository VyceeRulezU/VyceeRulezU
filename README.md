### 👋 About Me

I'm **Victor Ironali** — a Senior Product Designer & UX Engineer with 6+ years of experience 
designing and building SaaS products, web apps, and design systems that scale.

I run **(https://ironali.com)**, where I help startups and teams bridge 
the gap between design thinking and front-end engineering. My work spans user research, 
information architecture, prototyping, and shipping pixel-perfect, accessible interfaces 
in React, Tailwind, and TypeScript.

Previously designed and built at **Jobin.cloud** (Dublin) and **ERCAS** (London), 
delivering products used by 10M+ users across seven Nigerian state governments.

Currently building **[Governance Resource Hub](https://governanceresourcehub.com)** — 
a React 18 + Vite + Supabase platform for governance education.

- 🎨 I work at the intersection of design systems, UX engineering & AI-assisted product dev  
- 📖 Author of *Design Foundations for Vibe Coders*  
- 🌍 Based in Abuja, Nigeria  
- ⚡ I design it. Then I build it.
